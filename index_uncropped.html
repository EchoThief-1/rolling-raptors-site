Here's what
to expect

01

No wheelchair? No problem

If you use a wheelchair day-to-day, you're already equipped — no sport chair required. Curious to try one anyway? We keep a few on hand. Sticks, balls, and protective gear are provided either way.

02

Everyone's a beginner

This is a pick-up clinic, not a tryout. We teach the game from scratch every session, so it doesn't matter if this is your first Saturday or your fifteenth.

03

Bring your people

Friends, family, and caregivers are welcome on the court. Wheelchair lacrosse is built to be played together, not watched from the sideline.
